# FCE-GRPO results — 2026-07-29

## SmolLM2 pre-registered selection gate

The frozen bank was finalized before outcome inspection:

- model: `HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct`
- dataset: `openai/gsm8k`, test split, first 500 prompts
- frozen panels: 12 + 12 initial-policy rollouts per prompt
- independent candidate panel: 4 rollouts per prompt
- bank state: `partial=false`, `frozen_evidence_attached=true`,
  `gold_stored=false`

| Metric | FCE | Strict FCC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Random candidate accuracy | 26.05% | 26.05% |
| Method-selected accuracy | 48.40% | 41.05% |
| Oracle candidate accuracy | 55.00% | 55.00% |
| Gain over random | +22.35 pp | +15.00 pp |
| Gain 95% CI | [+19.60, +25.10] pp | [+12.75, +17.30] pp |
| `p(gain <= 0)` | 0 | 0 |
| Oracle gain captured | 77.20% | 51.81% |
| Informative group rate | 56.60% | 33.00% |
| Reinforcement event rate | 62.00% | 38.00% |
| Wrong given reinforcement | 24.03% | 8.42% |

FCE passed all pre-registered gates:

- oracle gain captured at least 70%: **pass**
- paired-bootstrap `p < 0.05`: **pass**
- informative group rate at least 30%: **pass**

Strict FCC failed the oracle-capture gate. Its lower wrong-target rate reflects
greater selectivity, but the signal was too sparse to capture the required
candidate-selection gain.

- dataset: `openai/gsm8k`, test split, first 500 prompts
- frozen panels: 12 + 12 initial-policy rollouts per prompt
- independent candidate panel: 4 rollouts per prompt
- bank state: `partial=false`, `frozen_evidence_attached=true`,

| Oracle candidate accuracy | 72.40% | 72.40% |

| Gain 95% CI | [+23.45, +29.05] pp | [+16.70, +21.45] pp |
| `p(gain <= 0)` | 0 | 0 |

FCE passed all three Qwen gates: 81.78% oracle-gain capture, paired-bootstrap

pre-registered 70% oracle-capture gate. Re-running both evaluators from the
sealed Qwen bank produced byte-identical result JSON hashes.

## Post-hoc paired FCE versus FCC comparison

This comparison was computed after the pre-registered gates and is therefore
corroborating evidence, not a new pass criterion.

On the same 500 independent candidate groups, FCE exceeded strict FCC by 7.35
percentage points. A paired 100,000-draw bootstrap gave a 95% interval of
[5.35, 9.45] percentage points with no non-positive draw. FCE was better on 61
groups, FCC was better on 13, and 426 were tied. A paired sign-flip test had no
randomized statistic at least as large as the observed statistic in 1,000,000
draws (add-one corrected one-sided estimate approximately `1e-6`).

On Qwen, FCE exceeded strict FCC by 7.20 percentage points. A paired
100,000-draw bootstrap gave a 95% interval of [5.15, 9.30] percentage points
with no non-positive draw. FCE was better on 66 groups, FCC was better on 16,
and 418 were tied. As above, this direct method comparison is post-hoc rather
than a pre-registered pass criterion.

## Online policy-learning gate

The online arm used a gold-free bank built from `train[:1000]`. Frozen evidence
was nonempty for 895 prompts. Both policies ran for 1,000 steps from
`HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct` with seed 42, K=4, learning rate 2e-5,
and deterministic greedy evaluation on untouched GSM8K test items 500:1000.

| Arm | Correct | Accuracy | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Untrained base | 148/500 | 29.60% | — |
| Matched trajectory-permutation control | 168/500 | 33.60% | +4.00 pp vs base |
| FCE-GRPO | 257/500 | 51.40% | +21.80 pp vs base |

FCE passed the pre-registered policy-learning gate:

- FCE minus base: +21.80 percentage points
- paired-bootstrap 95% CI: [+17.40, +26.20] percentage points
- `p(gain <= 0) = 0` over 50,000 draws
- FCE-only correct: 132; base-only correct: 23

## Matched attribution control

The unconditional control preserved each current K=4 group's exact FCE reward
multiset but deterministically applied a uniform random permutation to the four
trajectory assignments. Everything else—the starting model, frozen bank, seed,
schedule, optimizer, sampling parameters, reward density, and held-out prompt
order—was matched.

FCE passed the pre-registered attribution gate:

- FCE minus control: +17.80 percentage points
- paired-bootstrap 95% CI: [+13.20, +22.40] percentage points
- `p(gain <= 0) = 0` over 50,000 draws
- FCE-only correct: 123; control-only correct: 34

The final control was one clean uninterrupted step-0 run. Its 1,000-step history
contains all 200 expected log records, 100% frozen-bank coverage, 64.05% mean
nonzero-token advantage coverage, and maximum within-rollout advantage variance
`2.49e-15`. The final model weights are byte-identical to checkpoint 1000.

An earlier proposal to resume from checkpoint 200 was rejected before held-out
evaluation: its checkpoint-250 replay differed from the original trajectory in
trainer state, model weights, optimizer state, RNG state, and immutable metadata.
That run and every interrupted attempt are archived and excluded from attribution.

## Descriptive behavior audit

This audit was not a pre-registered gate:

| Arm | Parseable answers | Accuracy given parseable |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base | 68.20% | 43.40% |
| Matched control | 78.60% | 42.75% |
| FCE-GRPO | 100.00% | 51.40% |

On the 393 prompts where both FCE and the control produced parseable answers,
FCE accuracy was 53.18% versus 42.75% for the control. The learning gain is
therefore not solely explained by converting previously unparseable completions.

## Systematic wrong-consensus stress test — 2026-07-30

Four label-free safeguards were evaluated on the existing SmolLM2 selection
bank. None passed all three development gates. The nearest frontier was a
minimum of two occurrences in each frozen panel: it reduced
wrong-given-reinforcement by 29.77%, retained 77.63% of baseline selection gain,
and kept 42.20% of groups informative. The retained-gain target was 80%.

That rule was explicitly locked before inspecting its Qwen safeguard outcome.
On the untouched Qwen selection bank:

| Metric | Baseline FCE | Locked min-two safeguard |
|---|---:|---:|
| Selection gain over random | +26.25 pp | +22.40 pp |
| Selection-gain retention | — | 85.33% |
| Informative group rate | 72.00% | 56.60% |
| Wrong given reinforcement | 21.29% | 12.61% |
| Relative wrong-reinforcement reduction | — | 40.78% |

The locked Qwen replication passed all three gates: at least 25% relative
wrong-reinforcement reduction, at least 80% gain retention, and at least 30%
informative groups. This mitigates weak systematic consensus; it cannot solve
the case where every observable frozen panel statistic supports the same
diversely worded wrong answer.

## Claim boundary

These results establish cross-family candidate-selection viability and actual
verifier-free policy learning for SmolLM2 on the registered GSM8K setting.
Correct FCE reward alignment beats both the untrained policy and a matched
reward-multiset permutation control.

They additionally establish a locked cross-model replication of one
wrong-consensus safeguard on candidate selection. They do not yet establish
cross-model replication of full policy learning, policy learning beyond
canonicalizable numeric answers, step-local credit, or superiority to gold
GRPO and other verifier-free training baselines.
